SAMPLE FOOD TENDER: SUPPLY OF PACKAGED MILK AND READY-TO-EAT MEALS
Tender reference: FST/2026/017
Procuring entity: District Nutrition Programme
Delivery period: 12 months

SCOPE
Supply and delivery of pasteurised milk and ready-to-eat meals to public nutrition centres. Products must be safe, within shelf life, and suitable for children and vulnerable beneficiaries.

SUPPLIER ELIGIBILITY AND CERTIFICATES
The bidder shall submit a valid FSSAI licence, GST registration certificate, HACCP or ISO 22000 certificate, product liability insurance certificate, and certificate of analysis from an NABL accredited laboratory. Certificates must show the legal entity name, certificate number, scope, issue date, expiry date, and issuing body. The procuring entity may verify each certificate with the issuing authority before award.

QUALITY, TRACEABILITY, AND DELIVERY
Each consignment shall include batch traceability records, ingredient and allergen labelling, manufacturing date, expiry date, and a recall procedure. Milk shall be transported in a cold chain at 0-4 C with temperature logs. Frozen meal components shall remain at -18 C or below. Products shall have at least 75 percent of their shelf life remaining at delivery. Packaging shall be food-grade, sealed, and labelled in English and the local language.

INSPECTION AND TESTING
The procuring entity may collect random samples for laboratory testing. The supplier shall replace rejected or contaminated batches within 24 hours and report any food safety incident immediately.
